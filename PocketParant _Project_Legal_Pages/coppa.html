COPPA Direct Parental Consent & Child Protection Disclosure

Effective Date: August 10, 2026 | Document Ref: PP-COPPA-2026-V5
VERIFIABLE PARENTAL CONSENT GUARANTEE: PocketParent strictly adheres to the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506) and 16 CFR Part 312.

1. Direct Parental Consent Mechanism

PocketParent requires that all child profiles are created directly by a verified parent or legal guardian inside a parent-authenticated account protected by an email/password verification and a 4-digit Parent Security PIN lock. Children cannot independently create accounts or access the application without parent initialization.

2. Strict Child Data Minimization

We collect only the minimum information necessary to participate in chore gamification (e.g., first nickname, cartoon avatar, chore completion logs). We never require or request children's real full names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or social media handles.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Profiling

PocketParent contains ZERO third-party ad networks, zero targeted advertising, zero behavioural profiling, and zero data monetization. Child activity data is processed exclusively to maintain chore logs and reward points within the family unit.

4. Parental Right to Inspect and Delete Child Data

Parents retain full statutory rights under COPPA to inspect, modify, or permanently delete their child's profile data at any time via the PocketParent Android App under Settings > Family Profile, or by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@pocketparent.app.
