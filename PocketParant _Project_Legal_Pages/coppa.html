NOTICE TO PARENTS: Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA"), PocketParent must notify parents and obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from children under 13 years of age.

1. Information Collected Under Parental Consent

PocketParent collects only minimal information necessary to operate family chore tracking and reward gamification:

  • Child Nickname / Display Name: Selected by the parent (e.g., "Leo" or "Alex").
  • Avatar Choice: Cartoon character avatar selected by the family.
  • Chore Photo Proof: Photos taken by children showing finished chores. These photos are encrypted and stored in private cloud buckets accessible exclusively by the parent.

2. No Third-Party Advertising or Social Sharing

We do NOT allow third-party behavioral advertising to children, nor do we allow children to communicate publicly with strangers or share data outside their family network.

3. Parental Control & Revocation Rights

Parents may at any time:

  • Review the personal information collected from their child.
  • Refuse to permit further collection or use of their child's information.
  • Request the permanent deletion of their child's account and profile data.
