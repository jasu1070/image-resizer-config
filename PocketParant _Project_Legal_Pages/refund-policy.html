01 Google Play Store Managed Subscriptions

GOOGLE PLAY BILLING NOTICE: All subscriptions, in-app purchases, and payment transactions for PocketParent are handled directly by Google Play Store. Shravas Digital does not store or process credit card or banking details directly.

Because all transactions are processed through Google Play's secure billing system, all purchases, auto-renewals, subscription cancellations, and refunds are subject to Google Play Refund Policies.

02 How to Cancel Your Subscription

You can cancel your PocketParent Pro subscription at any time through your Google Play account:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile icon at the top right corner.
  3. Select Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  4. Select PocketParent and tap Cancel subscription.

Upon cancellation, you will retain access to your Pro features until the end of your current billing period.

03 How to Request a Refund via Google Play

If you made an accidental purchase or are dissatisfied with your subscription, you can request a refund directly from Google Play:

Within 48 Hours of Purchase:

You can request a refund directly through Google Play Order History:

  1. Go to Google Play Order History on a browser.
  2. Find your PocketParent purchase order.
  3. Select Request a refund or Report a problem and follow the instructions.

After 48 Hours of Purchase:

If more than 48 hours have passed, Google Play policy applies. You may also contact our support team directly with your Google Play Purchase GPA Order ID for assistance.
