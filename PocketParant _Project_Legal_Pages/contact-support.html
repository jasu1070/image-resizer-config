02 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

🔑 How do I reset my Parent Security PIN Lock?

If you forget your 4-digit Parent Security PIN, tap Forgot PIN on the lock screen inside the app to initiate account verification using your registered parent email address.

🗑️ How do I request account or data deletion?

You can delete your account directly inside the app via Settings > Security & Family Profile > Delete Account & Family Data, or email us at Jasu1070@gmail.com with subject line "ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST".

💳 How are subscriptions and billing handled?

All subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled directly through Google Play Store under Google Play billing policies. Learn more in our Refund Policy.

🔒 Is my family data shared or sold?

No. We maintain a zero-tolerance data policy. We never sell family or child data, show targeted ads, or share data with third-party data brokers. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
