Google Play Store Account & Data Deletion Request

In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, PocketParent account holders (parents/guardians) have the absolute right to request the complete, permanent deletion of their account, family profiles, child data, chore histories, and uploaded media.

How to Delete Your Account & Associated Data:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the PocketParent Android App > Go to Settings > Tap Security & Family Profile > Select Delete PocketParent Account & Family Data.
  2. Web Request: If you cannot access the mobile application, email our Data Protection Officer directly at privacy@pocketparent.app with the subject line "ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST" using your registered parent email address.

Data Handling Upon Deletion: All user credentials, child profiles, Firestore documents, Firebase Storage chore images, and authentication records are permanently erased from active databases within 30 days.
