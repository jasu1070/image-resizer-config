03 Google Play Account & Data Deletion Request

In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, parents and legal guardians have the absolute right to request the complete, permanent deletion of their account, family profiles, child data, and chore histories.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:
1. In-App: Open PocketParent > Settings > Security & Family Profile > Delete Account & Family Data.
2. Email Request: Send an email to Jasu1070@gmail.com with the subject line "POCKETPARENT ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST" using your registered parent email.

Upon verification, all account credentials, family data, child profiles, and uploaded images are permanently deleted from active cloud storage within 30 days.
