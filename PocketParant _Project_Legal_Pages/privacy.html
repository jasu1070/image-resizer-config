Privacy Policy & COPPA Compliance Notice

Effective Date: August 2, 2026 | Document Ref: PP-PRV-2026-V4
COPPA & GDPR-K GUARANTEE: We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR for Children, and CCPA. We NEVER sell child data, show targeted ads to children, or track children for behavioral profiling.

1. Information We Collect

When a parent registers an account, we collect basic account credentials (email address, hashed password, time zone). No personal credit card numbers are stored directly on our servers.

2. Child Profile Protection

Child profiles are created exclusively by the parent. We collect only display nicknames, cartoon avatar choices, and optional photo proof uploads for chore completion. Real full names or social security numbers are never required.

3. How Data Is Processed

Data is processed strictly to provide core features: calculating points, tracking chore progress, sending push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging, and verifying Parent Security PIN locks.

4. Subprocessor Controls

We process data through enterprise cloud providers (Google Cloud & Firebase) certified under SOC 2, ISO 27001, and COPPA data processing agreements. See our full Subprocessors List.

5. Parental Rights & Account Erasure

Parents retain complete authority over their family's data. You can inspect, modify, or permanently purge child profile data anytime in the app under Settings > Edit Family Profiles, or request full account erasure at privacy@pocketparent.app.
