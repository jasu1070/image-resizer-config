Global Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Last Updated: August 27, 2026 • Published by Shravas Digital

01 Global Privacy Policy & Child Safety Guarantee

GLOBAL CHILD PRIVACY GUARANTEE: PocketParent complies with COPPA (US), GDPR-K (EU/UK), CCPA/CPRA (California), and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (AADC). We NEVER sell personal or child data, show targeted ads to children, or sell data to third-party data brokers.

This Privacy Policy explains how PocketParent, developed and published by Shravas Digital ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you and your family use our mobile application and related services.

02 Information We Collect

PocketParent minimizes data collection to only what is strictly necessary to operate our family chore management and financial literacy platform:

  • Parent Account Information: Email address, display name, optional profile picture, encrypted security PIN hash, and authentication credentials (via Firebase Auth / Google Sign-In).
  • Child Profile Data: First name or nickname, cartoon avatar selection, assigned chores, point balances, and savings goal milestones created exclusively by the parent.
  • Chore Verification Media (Optional): Photos uploaded by family members as proof of completed household chores, stored in encrypted cloud storage (Firebase Storage).
  • Technical Diagnostics & Messaging Tokens: Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and crash logs to maintain app stability.

04 Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

PocketParent is designed for family use under strict parental supervision:

  • Verifiable Parental Consent: Children cannot independently sign up for an account. All child profiles are provisioned inside a parent-authenticated account protected by email/password verification and a 4-digit Parent Security PIN lock.
  • Data Minimization: We do not request or store sensitive personal information from children such as home addresses, phone numbers, or social security numbers.
  • Zero Advertising & Profiling: PocketParent contains ZERO third-party ad networks, zero targeted advertising, zero behavioral profiling, and zero data monetization.
  • Parental Inspection Rights: Parents possess absolute authority to review, modify, or permanently delete child profile data at any time.

05 Third-Party Services & Subprocessors

We partner with enterprise-grade cloud providers to deliver secure services:

  • Firebase Authentication & Google Sign-In: Secure user login & session verification.
  • Google Cloud Firestore & Firebase Storage: Encrypted database and media storage certified under ISO 27001 and SOC 2.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers transactional notifications (e.g., chore completed alerts).

06 Account & Data Deletion Request Portal

In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, parents and legal guardians have the absolute right to request the complete, permanent deletion of their account, family profiles, child data, and chore histories.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:
1. In-App Deletion: Open PocketParent > Settings > Security & Family Profile > Delete Account & Family Data.
2. Web Email Request: Send an email to Jasu1070@gmail.com with the subject line "POCKETPARENT ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST" using your registered parent email address.

Upon verification, all account credentials, family data, child profiles, and uploaded images are permanently erased from active cloud storage within 30 days.
