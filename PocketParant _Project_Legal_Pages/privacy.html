Global Privacy Policy & Data Safety Notice

Effective Date: August 10, 2026 | Document Ref: PP-PRV-2026-V5
GLOBAL CHILD PRIVACY GUARANTEE: PocketParent complies with COPPA (US), GDPR-K (EU/UK), CCPA/CPRA (California), and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (AADC). We NEVER sell personal or child data, show targeted ads to children, or sell data to third-party data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

PocketParent minimizes data collection to only what is necessary to operate our family chore management and financial literacy platform:

  • Parent Account Information: Email address, display name, optional profile picture, encrypted security PIN hash, and authentication credentials (via Firebase Auth / Google Sign-In).
  • Child Profile Data: First name or nickname, cartoon avatar selection, assigned chores, point balances, and savings goal milestones created exclusively by the parent.
  • Chore Verification Media (Optional): Photos uploaded by family members as proof of completed household chores, stored in encrypted cloud storage (Firebase Storage).
  • Technical Diagnostics & Messaging Tokens: Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and crash logs to maintain app stability.

2. Google Play Store Data Safety Declaration

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety requirements, below is our official data declaration matrix:

3. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) & GDPR-K

PocketParent is designed for family use under strict parental supervision:

  • Children cannot independently sign up for an account. All child profiles are provisioned inside a parent-authenticated account.
  • We do not request or store sensitive personal information from children such as home addresses, phone numbers, or social security numbers.
  • Parents possess absolute authority to review, modify, or permanently delete child profile data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services & SDK Disclosures

We partner with enterprise-grade cloud providers to deliver secure services:

  • Firebase Authentication & Google Sign-In: Secure user login & session verification.
  • Google Cloud Firestore & Firebase Storage: Encrypted database and media storage certified under ISO 27001 and SOC 2.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers transactional notifications (e.g., chore completed alerts).

5. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

We retain data only as long as your account remains active. Account holders have the absolute right to complete data deletion:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Settings > Security & Family Profile > Delete Account inside the PocketParent app.
  • Web Deletion Request: Visit our Account Deletion Request Portal or email privacy@pocketparent.app.

6. Parental & Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Users in the European Union, United Kingdom, California, and globally enjoy full statutory rights under applicable privacy laws: the right to access, rectify, port, or erase personal data, and the right to object to data processing.
