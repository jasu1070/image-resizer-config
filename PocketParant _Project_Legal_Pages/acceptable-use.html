Acceptable Use Policy & Community Safety Guidelines

Effective Date: August 10, 2026 | Document Ref: PP-AUP-2026-V5
SAFE FAMILY ENVIRONMENT GUARANTEE: PocketParent maintains a zero-tolerance policy against inappropriate content, child exploitation, and abusive behavior.

1. Safe Family Environment Standard

PocketParent is dedicated to creating a positive, uplifting family productivity environment. All uploaded media, chore titles, and family member display names must adhere to family-safe standards.

2. Strictly Prohibited Content & Behavior

Users may not upload, transmit, or store content that:

  • Is obscene, pornographic, sexually explicit, or harmful to minors.
  • Promotes violence, self-harm, illegal acts, or dangerous activities.
  • Infringes third-party intellectual property or copyright.
  • Attempts to bypass or hack Parent Security PIN locks or app security mechanisms.

3. Rules for Optional Chore Proof Photos

Photos uploaded to verify chore completion must strictly depict household tasks (e.g., a clean bedroom, washed dishes, completed homework) and must not contain sensitive personal identifiable information or inappropriate imagery.

4. Account Suspension & Termination

PocketParent Inc. reserves the right to immediately suspend or permanently ban accounts found in violation of this Acceptable Use Policy without prior notice.
