Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Effective Date: August 2, 2026 | Ref: PP-AUP-2026-V4

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules and restrictions governing user behavior, photo uploads, and interactions on PocketParent.

1. Prohibited Photo Uploads & Content Rules

PocketParent provides optional photo proof uploads for chore verification. Users are strictly prohibited from uploading content that contains:

  • Nudity, sexually suggestive images, or inappropriate attire.
  • Violence, weapons, illegal activities, or dangerous situations.
  • Personally identifiable documents (e.g., passports, social security cards, birth certificates, credit cards).
  • Copyrighted imagery owned by third parties without explicit authorization.

2. Anti-Abuse & Technical Security Standards

Users agree NOT to engage in any of the following technical abuses:

  • Attempting to bypass Parent Security PIN locks or tamper with custom claims roles.
  • Reverse-engineering, decompiling, or probing the mobile app binaries or cloud APIs.
  • Deploying automated scripts, scrapers, or bots to mint virtual Star Points or simulate task completions.
  • Interfering with infrastructure stability or attempting Denial of Service (DoS) attacks.

3. Violations & Account Termination Procedures

Failure to comply with this AUP may result in immediate suspension, permanent account deletion, and reporting to law enforcement authorities where applicable.
