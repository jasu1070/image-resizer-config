Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where is my attendance data stored?

A: Your data is stored securely on your local device using encrypted SharedPreferences. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud unless you manually trigger a backup export from Profile → Cloud Data Backup.

Q: How do I calculate Overtime (OT)?

A: Configure your OT Rate per Hour in the Salary Configuration screen. When marking attendance, enter OT hours for each day. Your monthly OT earnings are automatically calculated and included in your estimated net salary.

Q: How can I restore my Pro subscription?

A: Open Profile → Subscription & Plans and tap "Restore" in the upper right corner. This will reactivate your Pro features if you've previously purchased them.

Q: How do I export reports as PDF or CSV?

A: Go to the Reports tab → tap on any report type (Payslip, Attendance, Overtime, Statutory Ledger, or CSV). Tap "Save & Share" to save the file to your device's SalaryMate_Reports folder and open the share dialog.

Q: Can I use SalaryMate on multiple devices?

A: Yes! Export your data as a JSON backup from one device, then import it on another using Profile → Cloud Data Backup → Restore. Your Pro subscription must be restored separately on each device via the Play Store.

Q: How do I change the app language?

A: Go to Profile → App Language and select from 12 available languages including English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Chinese, Indonesian, and Turkish.

Q: Will my data be lost if I uninstall the app?

A: Yes. Since SalaryMate uses local-first storage, uninstalling removes all data. Always export a JSON backup before uninstalling to preserve your records.
