1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SalaryMate, a mobile application developed and published by Shravas Digital ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information.

SalaryMate is engineered with a Local-First Architecture. Salary, attendance, overtime, deduction, allowance, expense, and related records entered into the app are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

SalaryMate does use third-party services, including Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, to understand app usage and improve reliability. These services may collect app, device, installation, usage, crash, and diagnostic information as described below.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

SalaryMate may store information that you enter or generate within the app, including:

  • Salary and wage information
  • Overtime records
  • Attendance records
  • Deductions and allowance configurations
  • PF and ESIC calculation inputs
  • Expense records
  • Employee or company information entered by you
  • Generated payslip information
  • JSON backup data
  • CSV and PDF exports

This information is processed locally by the app. SalaryMate does not transmit your salary amounts, attendance records, overtime records, expense records, payslip contents, or employee/company names to our servers.

3. Backup and File Export

SalaryMate allows you to manually export or restore your app data using supported local files.

Depending on the feature you use, you may export:

  • JSON backup files
  • CSV files
  • PDF salary slips and reports

These exports are initiated by you and are saved to a location selected or controlled through the device file system. You are responsible for protecting exported files after they leave the app, including files stored in shared folders, cloud storage, email, or messaging applications.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

SalaryMate uses the following Google Firebase services:

Google Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand how users interact with SalaryMate and which features are used. Depending on app configuration, Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • App interaction and feature-usage events
  • Screen or app navigation information
  • App-instance identifiers
  • Device and app information
  • Session and usage information

SalaryMate is configured so that user-entered salary amounts, employee names, company names, expense descriptions, attendance records, and other financial record contents are not intentionally included in Analytics events or parameters.

Google Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics helps us identify crashes and stability problems. Crashlytics may collect:

  • Crash reports
  • Stack traces
  • Relevant application state
  • Device and app information
  • Crashlytics installation identifiers
  • Firebase installation identifiers
  • Other diagnostic information associated with a crash
SalaryMate does not intentionally place salary amounts, employee names, company names, expense descriptions, attendance records, or other financial record contents into Crashlytics logs, custom keys, user identifiers, or crash reports.

Firebase services may process information on Google's infrastructure and may use service providers/subprocessors as described in Google's applicable privacy documentation.

5. Data Transmission and Security

SalaryMate's locally stored salary, attendance, overtime, expense, and payslip records are not automatically uploaded to a cloud server.

Information transmitted by Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics is sent using the security mechanisms provided by Firebase/Google, including encrypted network transport where applicable. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the app and its third-party services. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Android Permissions

SalaryMate may request permissions or system capabilities necessary for its features:

  • Internet Access: Used for services such as Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, and for loading online legal or support pages.
  • File Access: SalaryMate may use system document mechanisms when you choose to export or restore JSON, CSV, or PDF files.

7. Data Retention

Local SalaryMate Data

Locally stored SalaryMate records remain on your device until you:

  • Delete them using the app's Reset All Data function;
  • Delete individual records where the app provides that functionality; or
  • Uninstall the application.

Uninstalling the application removes the app's locally stored data according to Android's application data management behavior.

Firebase Telemetry Retention

Firebase Analytics retention for user-level and event-level data is controlled by applicable Google Analytics/Firebase Analytics property settings (such as configurable user/event retention periods). Firebase currently states that Crashlytics retains crash stack traces, associated identifiers, and related crash data for approximately 90 days before beginning the removal process.

8. Data Deletion

SalaryMate does not allow users to create a SalaryMate user account and does not maintain a server-side account containing salary, attendance, or expense records. You can delete locally stored SalaryMate records through the app's in-app Reset All Data functionality.

Because SalaryMate does not maintain a user account or server-side database containing your salary, attendance, overtime, or expense records, there is no server-side SalaryMate account requiring account deletion. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics telemetry is handled separately by the applicable Google/Firebase services according to their retention and deletion mechanisms.
