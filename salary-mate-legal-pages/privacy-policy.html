1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the SalaryMate mobile application, developed and owned by Shravas Digital ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information generated or stored within the app.

SalaryMate is built on a Local-First Architecture. Your data stays on your device — we don't operate central servers storing your personal records.

2. Data Storage & Local-First Architecture

SalaryMate is engineered with a strict Local-First Architecture. Your attendance records, salary structures, allowance configurations, and overtime logs are stored exclusively on your device's secure local storage.

  • We do not operate central database servers storing your personal attendance or financial records.
  • Your financial figures, salary data, and PF/ESIC calculations remain 100% private to your local device.
  • No personal data is uploaded to any cloud server automatically.

3. Data Backup & User Export

Users can voluntarily export their data via encrypted JSON payloads for backup purposes, or save official payslip PDFs and CSV spreadsheets directly to their local file system. All exports are user-initiated and controlled.

4. Analytics & Third-Party SDKs

SalaryMate uses the following third-party services for app quality improvement:

  • Google Firebase Analytics — Collects anonymous usage statistics (screen views, feature usage) to help us improve the app experience. No personal financial data is transmitted.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — Collects anonymous crash reports and device diagnostics to ensure app stability across Android devices.
No personal identification, salary figures, attendance records, or financial amounts are ever transmitted in analytics or crash logs.

5. App Permissions

SalaryMate requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — Required for Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics diagnostics, and loading legal pages in the in-app WebView browser.
  • Storage (scoped) — Used only for saving PDF/CSV report exports to your device's documents folder.

6. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Uninstalling SalaryMate permanently deletes all stored attendance and salary data.
  • We do not retain any copies of your data after uninstallation.
  • Firebase Analytics data is retained for 14 months per Google's default policy, after which it is automatically deleted.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

SalaryMate is designed for working professionals and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used this app, please contact us and we will address your concerns.

8. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to:

  • Access your data — All data is visible and exportable directly from the app.
  • Delete your data — Uninstalling the app permanently removes all data, or use the in-app "Reset All Data" option.
  • Portability — Export your data via JSON backup or CSV export at any time.
