1 Introduction

Welcome to Photo: Image Resize & Compress ("App").

Photo: Image Resize & Compress is developed and operated by Shravas Digital.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you when using the App.

Privacy is one of the core principles of Photo: Image Resize & Compress. Unlike many image editing applications, your photos are processed entirely on your own device. We do not upload, access, analyze, store, or share your images through our servers.

By downloading or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to collecting as little information as possible.

This information does not include the content of your images.

3 How Your Images Are Processed

Photo: Image Resize & Compress performs image processing entirely on your device.

Features including, but not limited to:

  • Image resizing
  • Image compression
  • Format conversion
  • Image cropping
  • Batch processing
  • PDF creation
  • Other editing features

operate locally without uploading your images to our servers.

We cannot access, view, recover, or store your images because they never leave your device.

Temporary Local Cache

During processing — particularly for Batch Resize operations — the App may create temporary local cache copies of your images on your device. These cache copies are used solely to perform the requested processing task and are automatically cleared:

  • When a batch operation finishes,
  • When you stop a processing task, or
  • When the App is relaunched.

You may also clear this cache manually at any time from within the App. These temporary copies never leave your device, are never accessible to us, and clearing them does not affect your original photos.
